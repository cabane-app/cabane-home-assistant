Cabane 0.3.15

## What's new

- **Room cleaning:** start cleaning from a room's controls when your vacuum supports Home Assistant area cleaning. Includes robot selection and confirmation when cleaning is requested.
- **Animated vacuums:** cleaning robots gain floating motion, a tractor beam and dust effects.
- **More connected lights:** seven wall, ceiling and reading lamps now support Home Assistant bindings. Existing decorative instances remain compatible.
- **Expanded catalog:** new teak lounge furniture, jute rugs, cushions, candles, planters and flowering pots. More bedding and outdoor upholstery variants, plus four illustrated French landscapes.
- **Better Walk movement:** improved jumping over low obstacles, landing on supported furniture, and falling from raised platforms. Doors open visually on approach, without sending commands to Home Assistant.
- **Smoother navigation:** room tabs follow Home Assistant's area order, with improved overflow handling. Mouse and trackpad zoom respond more naturally, and manual camera adjustments remain intact after room focus.
- **Editor and rendering fixes:** switch top view back to 3D, clearer camera icons, and fixes for balcony slabs showing through ceilings.

## Compatibility and update

Workspace contract revision 10 remains unchanged. Existing homes require no migration.

Update through HACS, restart Home Assistant, then reload your browser or Companion app.

For manual installation, extract the complete `cabane.zip` release asset into `/config/custom_components/cabane/`, restart HA and reload the frontend.
