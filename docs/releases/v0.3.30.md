Cabane 0.3.30 updates the Home Assistant panel to workspace contract revision 42.

## Contract changes and upgrade impact

### Contract revision 40: Allow walls to lean (leanMeters)

- No saved fields or IDs change for existing projects. Walls may now store leanMeters; older frontends and Home Assistant panels reject projects that use it.

### Contract revision 41: Support bounded elevation areas, local ceiling overrides, surface-relative items and same-storey stairs.

- Older clients must update before opening projects containing elevation settings, surface-relative placements or between-surfaces stairs. Existing projects keep their original elevations.

### Contract revision 42: Allow walking up surface changes of up to 40 cm.

- No stored fields change. Walk stepping and accessibility diagnostics now share a 40 cm threshold instead of 30 cm; older panels retain the smaller threshold.

## Update

Update Cabane in HACS, restart Home Assistant, then reload your browser or Companion app. Keep saved workspaces unchanged; no manual JSON edits are needed. Older clients continue to block unsupported data to protect it.

For manual installation, extract the complete cabane.zip release asset into /config/custom_components/cabane/, restart HA and reload the frontend.

This release passed producer verification, native-panel browser checks, representative-scale checks, integration tests and package validation with synthetic HA data. Live household and Companion acceptance are separate.
