Cabane 0.3.20 updates the Home Assistant panel to workspace contract revision 18.

## Contract changes and upgrade impact

### Contract revision 15: Support mixed light sources and remove fixed lighting cutoffs

- Existing point and strip declarations remain readable without migration. Mixed explicit source layouts and expanded sample counts require this updated frontend and matching native panel. Older clients retain unsupported-data protection. No asset, HA binding or backend migration is required.

### Contract revision 16: Keep misplaced items editable and add explicit placement repairs

- Existing homes remain compatible. The updated panel treats geometric placement diagnostics as warnings, checks scaled bounds before Arrange edits, and offers per-copy repairs in Issues. Older panels can still block a home on legacy device contact errors. No data or asset migration is required.

### Contract revision 17: Support independent furniture and device sensor readings with scene visibility and TV switch controls

- Existing homes remain supported. Furniture entitySlots and slot presentation.showInScene require the matching updated frontend and Home Assistant panel. Mixed model lighting and placement repairs from revisions 15 and 16 remain supported. Older clients retain unsupported-field protection.

### Contract revision 18: Add virtual room boundaries without physical geometry or collision

- Existing homes remain supported. Homes using WallSpec.virtual require the matching updated frontend and Home Assistant panel; older clients keep unsupported fields blocked. Mixed lighting, placement repairs and independent entity readings from revisions 15 through 17 remain supported.

## Update

Update Cabane in HACS, restart Home Assistant, then reload your browser or Companion app. Keep saved workspaces unchanged; no manual JSON edits are needed. Older clients continue to block unsupported data to protect it.

For manual installation, extract the complete cabane.zip release asset into /config/custom_components/cabane/, restart HA and reload the frontend.

This release passed producer verification, native-panel browser checks, representative-scale checks, integration tests and package validation with synthetic HA data. Live household and Companion acceptance are separate.
